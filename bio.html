Sofia Seithalil is a multidisciplinary documentary artist, co-founder, and director of visual communications at the Qizlar collective.

Sofia was born in the small town of Uray in Siberia, spent her early childhood between Tashkent, the Samarkand region, and Kazakhstan, and has lived in Tashkent for as long as she can remember. The main inspiration for her work comes from observing and living within a community of like-minded people and friends.
